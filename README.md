## 🕹️ About

Graveyard Keeper 2 is a darkly humorous medieval management RPG set in a neglected town overrun by undead townsfolk. Restore districts, manage a customizable graveyard, automate production, build businesses, and turn the town’s zombie crisis into a profitable enterprise. Developed by Lazy Bear Games and published by tinyBuild, the game is scheduled for release on September 22, 2026.

Graveyard Keeper 2 is for players who enjoy dark comedy, crafting, automation, town management, exploration, and unconventional simulation RPGs.

## ✨ Features

- 🏘️ **Restore the Town** — Reclaim districts, rebuild infrastructure, and guide the town toward prosperity.
- ⚰️ **Manage the Graveyard** — Design and expand your graveyard with graves, monuments, benches, and columbaries.
- 🧟 **Command Undead** — Lead an undead army into battle and defend the town from zombie threats.
- ⚙️ **Automate Production** — Create efficient production chains that keep resources and businesses running.
- 🏪 **Build Businesses** — Reopen cafés, shops, and other businesses to create markets for your goods.
- 🌳 **Gather and Craft** — Explore the surrounding world, gather materials, and develop useful technologies.
- 💰 **Turn Problems Into Profit** — Supply the townsfolk, unlock valuable resources, and build a thriving local economy.
- 🌫️ **Explore a Living World** — Experience a fully 3D medieval fantasy world with dynamic weather, lighting, and shadows.
